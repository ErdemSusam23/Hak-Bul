1. Introduction

1. Introduction

🎯 1.1 Problem Statement

Access to legal information in Turkey is economically and practically difficult. Attorney fees are often unaffordable; hundreds of statutes, thousands of regulations, and ever-updating Supreme Court decisions make navigation nearly impossible for ordinary citizens.

  • 💸High cost of professional legal consultation
  • 📚Complex, fragmented Turkish legislation (33+ core laws)
  • 🤖No reliable AI tool for Turkish legal domain
🚀 1.2 Purpose & Target Audience

Hak-Bul enables citizens to ask legal questions in plain Turkish and receive cited, source-backed answers drawn from Turkish legislation and Yargıtay (Supreme Court) decisions.

👥 Primary Users

Citizens with everyday legal questions (tenancy, employment, consumer rights)

💡 Solution

RAG pipeline · 75,789 legal chunks · 14 law categories · Groq Llama-3.3-70b

1.4 Literature Research
04
1. Introduction

1.4 Literature Research

💬 General Purpose LLMs (GPT-4)

Provide robust reasoning and natural language generation. While highly capable, they require domain-specific grounding (RAG) to ensure accuracy and provide exact citations for Turkish legal texts.

📚 Domain-Specific Models (LEGAL-BERT)

Pioneering works by Chalkidis et al. established the effectiveness of specialized embeddings for legal NLP, primarily focusing on classification and representation in English contexts.

🏛️ Traditional Legal Portals

Official platforms like Mevzuat.gov.tr offer comprehensive, authoritative archives of Turkish law, relying on exact-match keyword search paradigms for document retrieval.

🏆 Hak-Bul's Contribution

Synergizes the reasoning power of LLMs with a custom RAG architecture. It utilizes semantic embeddings over a vast Turkish legal corpus to deliver accurate, context-aware, and fully cited responses.

1.5 Innovative Aspects
05
1. Introduction

1.5 Innovative Aspects

🔍 8-Step RAG Pipeline
🔄

Query categorization (14 legal domains) → rewriting → dual-collection Qdrant retrieval → cross-encoder reranking → Llama generation. Category penalty suppresses off-topic results by 50%.

📡 Real-Time SSE Streaming
⚡

Answers stream token-by-token via Server-Sent Events, providing a near-instant response feel powered by Groq's LPU inference (Llama-3.3-70b-versatile, 128k context window).

🛡️ Reliable Guardrails
🛡️

Implements ethical AI safeguards for sensitive legal matters. Features continuous weak-query logging to monitor low-confidence retrievals and actively expand the legal corpus.

📄 Beyond Chat
🧩

PDF document analysis & comparison, legal document template generation (ReportLab), community forum with lawyer-verified replies, and an admin analytics dashboard — all in one platform.
